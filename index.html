About this project

A digital interactive model that simulates heat transfer in a thermodynamic system, so learners can see and measure sensible heat, latent heat and gas behaviour while applying the First Law of Thermodynamics and the Kinetic Theory of Gases with the Gas Laws.

  • Experiment 1: Heating curve (Q = mcΔT, Q = mL)
  • Experiment 2: Piston and First Law (ΔU = Q − W)
  • Experiment 3: Gas laws (PV = nRT)
  • Built with HTML, JavaScript and Three.js (3D and 2D views)
